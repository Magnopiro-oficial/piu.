# Piu privacy policy

[Português](PRIVACIDADE.md)

Piu has no server, asks for no account and keeps your data nowhere but on your device.

## What stays on your device
- Chats, contacts, identities, keys and the wallet stay on your phone or computer, encrypted.
- No phone number or e-mail is required.
- Uninstalling the app erases everything. Without a backup you made yourself, nothing can be recovered.

## What leaves your device
- **Messages and calls** travel end-to-end encrypted to nearby devices (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct, local Wi-Fi, LoRa radio) and, through them, to their destination. Relays see neither sender, content nor group.
- **Lunes wallet**, when online: the app queries the public Lunes network nodes (`*.lunes.io`) and the `lunes-squid.lunes.io` indexer to show balance and history. These services see your IP and the address queried.
- **New version alerts**, when online and turned on (Settings): at most once a day, the app checks the releases published on GitHub (`api.github.com`), which sees your IP.

## Google components on Android
- **Wi-Fi Direct** uses Nearby Connections, from Google Play Services. Turn Wi-Fi Direct off in the Network tab to avoid it.
- **QR scanning** uses Google ML Kit, which may send technical usage data to Google, under Google's terms.

## What Piu does not do
- No ads.
- No analytics or tracking of its own.
- No selling or sharing of data: the project does not have your data.
