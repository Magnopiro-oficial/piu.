HOW IT WORKS

Every device is a piece of the network.

If the recipient is far away, your message hops between phones, routers and antennas in the neighborhood until it arrives. If no one is nearby, it waits, stored and encrypted, until a route appears.

PRIVACY

Whoever carries it can’t read it.

Every message leaves your device encrypted. Phones and routers along the way only carry a sealed envelope and keep as little as possible on record.

What we don’t promise
Absolute anonymity. Timing, signal patterns and physical observation can allow correlation. The anonymous identity and metadata options reduce that risk without eliminating it.
