Project overview
WHY

The Problem

01

Every message goes through a server

Today's messengers route every word through a company's data center. If it goes down or gets blocked, everyone goes quiet.

02

No internet, no conversation

Blackouts, floods, packed stadiums, rural areas. The moments people most need to talk are when the network fails.

03

Your metadata belongs to someone else

Even with encryption, a central server knows who you talk to, when, and from where. Signing up usually needs a phone number.

WHAT

Meet Piu

An offline-first messenger. Messages hop from device to device — over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or radio — until they reach the person you wrote to.

cloud_off No central server
lock End-to-end encrypted
schedule_send Waits and forwards when there's no route yet

Five Ways to Connect

TRANSPORT GOOD FOR CARRIES
Bluetooth LE Phones in the same room or street Text
Wi-Fi Direct Fast phone-to-phone transfers Text, files, the app itself
Wi-Fi Mesh Routers covering a block or neighborhood Everything
LoRa radio Long distances, very little bandwidth Text and alerts only
USB / Ethernet Wired links between nodes Everything

Photos and files never go over LoRa — they pause and wait for a faster link.

PART 03

Network & Economy

Providers, credits, LUNES and what comes next

03 · NETWORK & ECONOMY

Who Runs the Network

Anyone can be a provider: plug in equipment, relay traffic for others, earn credits.

home Home Node A small box at home relaying for the neighbors Earns credits
hub Super Node Always-on, several radios, stores and forwards for an area Earns credits
cell_tower Local Gateway Links distant meshes when any connection is available Earns credits
router Access Point A regular router, no Piu software, linked to a parent node Earnings go to the parent
