Piu is a messenger where messages travel from phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted, with no central server and no need for the internet.

Phones, routers and small computers form a mesh over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct, local Wi-Fi and LoRa radio. Each device carries encrypted envelopes a little further until they reach their destination. Nobody in the middle can read, filter or switch off a conversation, because there is no middle.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
• At concerts, protests, in rural areas, on boats, during blackouts or when the network is down: the people nearby already are the network.
• A message with no route waits in the mesh for up to 48 hours and moves on when a path appears.
• The app picks the best link for each message and switches when one fails.

REAL PRIVACY
• No phone number, no e-mail, no server account.
• Two identities: a public one for people you know and an anonymous one for strangers, with separate keys.
• End-to-end encryption (X25519, Ed25519, Double Ratchet). Relays see neither sender, content nor group.
• Your data stays on your device, encrypted. No ads.

FEATURES
• Chats, groups, channels and communities, with community alerts.
• Encrypted voice calls, between phones and with Piu Desktop.
• Photos and files.
• Network map with your neighbors and the links that reach each one.
• Node mode: your phone helps carry other people's messages.
• LoRa radio (experimental): Meshtastic and the Piu radio.
• Built-in Lunes wallet.
• Share the app without internet: anyone who has Piu can send it to you over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct.

WHY THE APP ASKS FOR THESE PERMISSIONS
• Bluetooth and nearby Wi-Fi devices: find and talk to nearby devices. You choose in the Network tab which links stay on.
• Location (Android 12 or older only): the system requires it to scan for Bluetooth devices. Piu does not read your location.
• Microphone: voice calls.
• Camera: scan a contact's QR code.
• Notifications and foreground service: alert you to messages and calls and keep node mode running.
